\section{Benchmarks}
\label{sec:benchmark}

\subsection{STED: synthetic thermal event detection}
STED comprises 124 six-second $640{\times}480$ \SI{30}{\hertz} sequences
(100 train / 24 val) from our scene model with randomized illumination
(day/night), object count/size/speed/contrast, microbolometer time
constant, NETD, PRNU, and AGC regime (one quarter of sequences with strong
AGC corruption). Ground-truth boxes and full physics parameters are stored
per frame, enabling exact slice attribution and sensitivity analysis.

\subsection{FLIR-STED: real thermal video events}
\label{sec:flirsted}
We convert the eight continuous sequences of the FLIR ADAS v2 video test
set (3{,}145 frames, COCO labels, six retained classes) to event streams
with the default pipeline (AGC de-flicker, $4\times$ linear interpolation,
v2e mode). Five sequences form the train split and three the val split.
This is, to our knowledge, the first public event-stream conversion of
continuous FLIR video with labels.

\subsection{HIT-UAV events}
The five HIT-UAV sample videos (1{,}923 frames, \SI{7.5}{\hertz}) are
converted at $8\times$ upsampling with and without AGC de-flickering,
providing a permissively licensed real-data anchor and the interpolation
audit of Sec.~\ref{sec:e1}.
